Questions we get asked

How long does delivery take?

Orders reach most cities in 3 to 5 working days. Delivery is free above Rs. 5,000 and Rs. 250 below that. Store pickup is always free.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes. You can also pay with JazzCash, Easypaisa or a card. We confirm every order on WhatsApp before dispatch.

How do exchanges work?

Message us within 7 days of delivery with your order ID. If the piece is unworn with tags, we send a replacement size or style.

Where is my order?

Open the Track order page and enter your order ID. Each stage updates as your parcel moves.

Do you offer custom stitching?

On selected suits, yes. Ask on WhatsApp with your measurements. Custom pieces take 7 to 10 days.
