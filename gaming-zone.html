Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Send us a message below.

How do I register for the tournament?

Select "Register your team", enter your team name and captain details, and submit the form. You'll get a confirmation code straight away.

Is Gaming Zone free to join?

Yes. Creating an account and joining the community is free. If a tournament has an entry fee, it is shown on the tournament page before you sign up.

Which devices can I play on?

Most titles run on PC, and many are also available on console and mobile. Each game page lists supported platforms.

How are prizes paid out?

Winners are paid within 14 days of the final, after a quick identity check. Prize money is split among team members as the captain decides.
